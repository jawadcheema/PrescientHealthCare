FAQ

Questions We Get Asked

+What exactly does offshoring our billing change day to day?

Your front desk still checks patients in and your providers still document as they do now. Everything after that — eligibility, coding review, submission, posting, follow-up and appeals — moves to our team. You keep your existing practice management system and clearinghouse; we work inside them rather than asking you to migrate.

+How is our patient data protected?

We operate under signed Business Associate Agreements and a HIPAA compliance framework covering access controls, audit logging, encrypted transmission and staff training. Access is role-based and limited to the people assigned to your account. We are equally happy to work within your own security requirements where they are stricter.

+Will an offshore team understand our payers and specialty?

Our staff are trained on US payer rules and stay current on CMS guidance, commercial payer policy, AMA coding standards and HIPAA. Each account is assigned a consistent team that learns your specialty, your payer mix and your fee schedule rather than rotating between clients.

+How quickly does the transition happen?

Most practices are running fully with us inside two to four weeks. We start with the free revenue audit, then run parallel for a short period so nothing drops while credentials, EDI enrollments and workflows are handed over.

+What does this actually cost compared with billing in-house?

Offshoring removes the salary, benefits, turnover and software overhead of an in-house billing function, and the fractional CFO model delivers executive financial oversight without a full-time hire. Pricing is discussed after the audit, once we can both see the real volume and the recoverable revenue involved.
